feat(notebase): auto-generate flashcards and add read-aloud

New rows (including dictionary saves) now automatically become flashcards for
existing templates, so newly saved words immediately show up in review as new
cards. The notebase rows, card list, and review screen also gain a speak button
that pronounces the word using the extension's text-to-speech settings.
